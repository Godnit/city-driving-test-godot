# City Drive Missions v1 — Third-party assets

## 3D model

- **Toyota Corolla AE86 Trueno** by **Lexyc16**
- Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/toyota-corolla-ae86-trueno-fe02fba6302e450ea8424591493341ea
- License: Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY)
- Download mirror: the public Objaverse copy of the downloadable Sketchfab model.
- Used for the player vehicle and a lightweight traffic variant.

The roads, barriers, traffic lights, lamps, road markings, sidewalks, and buildings in v1 are original procedural geometry generated by the Godot project. No models from cartoon-asset sites are used.
